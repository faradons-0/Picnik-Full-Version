## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Picnik** is an innovative online photo editor designed specifically for Firefox users, allowing you to enhance your images directly from your browser without the hassle of installing separate software. The **latest version** of Picnik offers a comprehensive range of editing tools that make it easy for anyone to elevate their photos. Enjoy **full functionality** with features that include collage creation, photo filters, and much more, all while keeping your experience seamless within Firefox.

## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Easy Importing** — Quickly import images from your own collection or directly from URLs, including integration with Picasa, Flickr, and PhotoBucket.
- **Comprehensive Editing Tools** — Resize, rotate, and flip photos; adjust tones, brightness, and focus; and apply red-eye correction with ease.
- **Layer Support** — Work with layers to create complex edits and stunning compositions.
- **Collage Maker** — Create beautiful photo collages effortlessly with a variety of templates and presets.
- **Text and Frame Addition** — Personalize your images by adding text and decorative frames.

### Advanced Features:
- **Screenshot Capture** — Take screenshots directly from your browser and edit them using Picnik's tools.
- **Wide Palette of Effects** — Utilize a range of photo filters and adjustments to enhance your photographs creatively.
- **User-Friendly Interface** — Designed for ease of use, ensuring that anyone can enhance their photos without advanced knowledge.
